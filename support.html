About Pinple

Pinple lets you create layered photo collages — combine multiple background photos with a cutout portrait on top, all in a style that's trending on social platforms.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a collage?

Open the app, tap the canvas, select background photos (multi-select supported), then add a cutout portrait on top. Adjust positions, sizes, and layers as needed.

What photo permissions does the app need?

Pinple requires access to your photo library to read and import photos (including Live Photos) for collage creation. We do not collect, upload, or share any of your photos or personal data.

Does Pinple collect my data?

No. Pinple does not collect any user data. All processing happens locally on your device. For details, please see our Privacy Policy.

Which devices and iOS versions are supported?

Pinple supports iPhone running iOS 16.0 or later.

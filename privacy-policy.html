Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-12  ·  Pinple by wang kexiang

This Privacy Policy describes how the Pinple app ("Application") handles your information. Pinple is a free photo collage application for iOS.

Information We Collect

Pinple is designed to work entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers.

  • Photos you select are processed locally on your device only and are never uploaded to any server.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, location, or device identifiers.
  • We do not use any analytics or crash-reporting services that transmit data off your device.
  • We do not track your usage behavior.

Photo Library Access

Pinple requests access to your photo library solely to allow you to select photos for creating collages. This permission is used only when you actively choose a photo within the app. We do not access, scan, or analyze photos without your explicit action.

Third-Party Services

Pinple does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services. No user data is shared with third parties.

Children's Privacy

Pinple does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Data Retention

Since Pinple does not collect or store personal data on any server, there is no user data for us to retain or delete. All photo processing results remain on your device under your control.

Security

Because all data remains on your device, your information is protected by your device's own security features, including Face ID, Touch ID, and iOS system protections.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the Application constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
